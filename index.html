Microbot Swarm - Battery & Charging Dock Simulation

Dock (bottom-left) has 4 charging pads. When all pads are busy, low-battery bots queue nearby and move to a pad as soon as one frees up. The ring around each bot shows its battery level (green/yellow/red); a brief green flash means it just recovered a tiny bit of charge from regenerative braking. Time is compressed ~60x so a realistic ~4-hour run cycle (default drain setting) plays out in about 4 real minutes; the "Sim. time elapsed" stat tracks simulated time, not wall-clock time.
